Shakshouka

Description

Shakshouka is a dish that plays with eggs as the main and vegetables as support. it is delicous , leaves a good taste and healthy!

Ingredients

  • dish of eggs poached in a sauce of tomatoes
  • olive oil
  • peppers
  • onion
  1. Sauté vegetables: Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add a diced onion and a diced red bell pepper. Cook for 5 to 8 minutes until soft.
  2. Add spices and garlic: Stir in minced garlic, 1 teaspoon cumin, and ½ teaspoon paprika. Cook for about 30 to 60 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Simmer the tomato base: Pour in a 28-ounce can of crushed or diced tomatoes with a pinch of salt and pepper. Simmer over low heat for 15 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  4. Create wells for eggs: Use a spoon to make 4 to 6 small spaces (wells) in the simmering tomato sauce.
  5. Poach the eggs: Gently crack an egg into each well. Cover the skillet with a lid. Cook on medium-low heat for 5 to 8 minutes until the egg whites are set and the yolks remain soft.
  6. Garnish and serve: Remove from heat, top with fresh chopped parsley or cilantro and optional crumbled feta cheese. Serve warm with crusty bread or pita.
