Koshary

Description

This is an ancient cultural Egyption dish.It's main attracting point is that it is a more a nutrious mix with a spicey sauce that make you want to try it even more although it leaves a bad smell in your mouth post-eating.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • pasta
  • macaroni
  • rice
  • brown lintils
  • checkpeas
  • heavy garlicky tomato sauce
  • garlick vinegar(optional)
  • very hot sauce(optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the Components
    • Crispy Onions: Slice onions thin. Toss them in a little flour. Fry them in hot oil until golden and crisp. Set them aside on a paper towel. Save the leftover onion oil for the rice and pasta.
    • Lentils: Soak brown lentils in water for 30 minutes, then boil them with salt and cumin until tender. Drain and set aside.
    • Rice and Vermicelli: Brown dry vermicelli noodles in the saved onion oil. Add rinsed short-grain rice, water, and salt. Cover and cook until fluffy. Mix in some of the cooked lentils or keep them separate.
    • Pasta: Boil small elbow macaroni and broken spaghetti until tender. Drain and toss with a bit of oil so it does not stick.
    • Chickpeas: Warm cooked or canned chickpeas with a pinch of cumin and salt.
  2. Make the Sauces
    • Tomato Sauce (Salsa): Sauté minced garlic in oil, add tomato paste, fresh tomato juice or sauce, cumin, coriander, salt, and a splash of vinegar. Simmer until thick.
    • Vinegar Sauce (Da'a): Cook minced garlic in a little oil for 30 seconds. Add white vinegar, water, lemon juice, salt, and cumin. Simmer briefly.
  3. Assemble the Dish
    • Layering: Put a base layer of rice and lentils. Add a layer of mixed pasta.
    • Toppings: Spoon warm chickpeas over the pasta. Add a generous pile of crispy fried onions on top.
    • Serving: Drizzle the tomato sauce and vinegar sauce over the bowl just before eating.
